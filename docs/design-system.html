2. Layout rules

  1. L1 · Field row. Every setting is one row: label column 220px, control column the rest. Below 960px the row stacks. Only the template picker and choice cards with more than four options take the whole row, label on top.
  2. L2 · One control width. Inputs, selects, textareas, descriptions, value pickers, image pickers, colour sets and choice grids all stop at --puiw-control (560px). Their start and end edges line up from one row to the next.
  3. L3 · Compound controls fill it exactly. When a control is made of parts (preview + URL + button + clear), the stretchy part takes the leftover width so the group ends on the same edge as a plain input.
  4. L4 · Choice grids. Equal columns across the full control width: 1 choice → 1 column, 2 → 2, 3 → 3, 4 → 2×2, 5 or more → rows of 3. Never let the grid size itself to its text.
  5. L5 · Label alignment. A label sits level with a one-line control and at the top of a tall one (textarea, card grid, switch board, multi choice).
  6. L6 · One height per row. Everything placed side by side in one control shares a height: 40px for inputs and buttons, 34px for search.
  7. L7 · Switch boards span the panel in a grid of cards, with a search box once there are more than five.
  8. L8 · Panels pad 24px. Groups inside a panel are separated by 16px and a hairline.
  9. L9 · One content edge. Field rows, switch boards and tab bars stop at label + gap + control (796px), so every block in a panel ends on the same edge. The template picker is the one block that runs to the panel's edges.
  10. L10 · No long sections. A section with more than about ten settings is split into tabs (title field puiw_tabs, each field puiw_tab). Each tab holds one subject. The settings that change the look most (colours) come first in their tab.

3. Text rules

  1. T1 · Card labels ≤ 24 characters, two to four words, no verb. Name the choice and leave out the "show" or "use".
  2. T2 · Hints are one line (about 90 characters at most) and say what the setting changes, never repeat the label.
  3. T3 · Placeholders on search boxes are exactly «جست‌وجو…» / "Search…".
  4. T4 · No "Show" prefix on a switch inside a show/hide board; the board already means show.
  5. T5 · Every string translated into Persian before it ships. Digits in prose follow the site language; technical values (URLs, codes, colours) stay Latin and LTR.

4. Field row

روی همه‌ی فاکتورها چاپ می‌شود

اگر خالی بماند نام سایت استفاده می‌شود.

L1, L2, L5. Label 13px/500, tip 12px muted. Rows divide with a #f0f0f1 hairline and 12px padding.

5. Buttons

40px tall, radius 7. Primary is filled accent, secondary is outlined accent, destructive is a 40×40 square in danger tints. A button never shrinks below the inputs beside it.

6. Choice cards

به فاکتور آنلاین لینک می‌شود

L4 and T1. A select with up to four choices becomes radio cards; a multiple choice with up to sixteen becomes ticked cards. Selected: accent border and soft fill.

7. Switch board

L7, T4. Every checkbox is a card with a 36×20 switch whose knob is centred vertically. Every card keeps its hint.

8. Search box

One style everywhere (settings sidebar, switch boards, template picker, value picker): 34px tall, radius 8, #c9ccd0 border on canvas, 16px icon at the start, accent focus ring, placeholder per T3. Pills (radius 999) are only for filter chips and switches.

9. Value picker

درج مقدار
سفارش

Under every text field that takes placeholders. Grouped, with a search box past eight values, and folded past twelve. A click inserts at the cursor (or appends once in list mode) and flashes green.

10. Colour set

Schemes are cards (name + three chips) above labelled primary, secondary and tertiary pickers, all in one row, at the top of their tab. Picker names are a div, never a label wrapping the input: the WordPress picker hides that text. Template previews are tinted to the chosen primary colour and carry a three chip strip.

11. Image picker

L3 and L6: 40px preview, URL field that stretches, choose button, 40×40 clear. The group ends on the same edge as the input above it.

12. PRO

♛ حرفه‌ای

Gold is reserved for PRO. A PRO field or template carries a 17px crown; on a template card the crown sits in the corner opposite the selected tick. The toolbar shows a PRO badge when PRO is active and a Get PRO link when it is not, never a version number.

13. Notes and states

پیامک با همان سرویس‌دهنده‌ای ارسال می‌شود که در افزونه‌ی WP SMS تنظیم شده است.

Information notes are accent tinted. Warnings use #fffaf0 with #f0c674 borders; errors use the danger tints. Filled values show as green pills (#edf7ee on #1a7a2e).

14. Dialogs, toasts and toolbars

  1. C1 · Dialogs are assets/admin/puiw-dialog.js, never jquery-confirm's look: white card, radius 12, title 16px, content, a footer strip with buttons at the end. The last button does the thing and is filled (danger red for destructive). Esc and a click outside close it.
  2. C2 · Toasts are a small dark card at the bottom centre with a coloured strip on the start edge: green saved, red failed, accent working. One line, an icon when it succeeded.
  3. C3 · Toolbars sit once, under what they act on, above a hairline: primary first, then secondary, destructive last in a red outline. 40px buttons with an icon and a label.
  4. C4 · Inline actions inside a field (save a tracking code) are a 28px button at the field's end; the field reserves room for it.
  5. C5 · Diagnostics put every value on the same edge whatever its script.
